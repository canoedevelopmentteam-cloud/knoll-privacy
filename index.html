1. Who we are

Knoll is a game made by Canoe Development ("Canoe Development", "we", "us"). Contact: canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com. This policy explains what data the Knoll app for Android, distributed on Google Play, handles.

Questions? Email canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • Knoll has no account system of its own and no server of its own. We do not ask you to create a Canoe Development account.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Knoll also keeps a copy in your Play Games cloud save (Saved Games). You can play without Play Games; your progress then stays on your device.
  • Google processes data when you use Play Games Services. Google's own privacy policy covers that.
  • Knoll sells nothing: there are no in-app purchases and no payments.
  • Knoll shows rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one) through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect device identifiers (including your advertising ID), your IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data. The ads SDK starts only once Google's consent tool says ads may be requested. Personalized ads are used only for players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland who consent; everyone else, including players in the US, gets non-personalized ads. See Section 5.
  • Knoll includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

4. Cloud save and Google Play Games Services

Knoll uses Google Play Games Services for one thing: saving your game progress to your Play Games Saved Games, so it can be restored on another device or after a reinstall.

When Knoll starts, it checks whether you are already signed in to Play Games (Play Games may sign you in automatically if you use a Play Games profile). Knoll has no sign-in button of its own. If you are not signed in, or you are offline, nothing is read from or written to the cloud.

When you are signed in, the cloud save contains your Knoll game data only: levels completed, stars and scores, talent points and ranks, the level in progress (board, stored pieces), your in-game tokens and Store items (game items, not money), Endless mode best score and run, and in-game achievements. It contains no name, email, or contact details. If the cloud copy is newer than the copy on your device, Knoll restores it (and keeps a backup of the device copy on the device).

When you are signed in, Google may process data such as your Play Games player ID and gamer profile, your saved game data, analytics and diagnostics that Google's Play Games SDK collects automatically, and device and service information Google needs to run the service. Your saved game data is stored in your Google account. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Play Games profile settings you choose. Canoe Development does not control how Google processes this data.

Knoll does not use Play Games achievements, leaderboards, or friends features. Knoll's achievements are in-game only (on your device and in your cloud save).

5. Ads

Knoll shows ads through Google AdMob, Google's ad network, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Each time Knoll opens, Google's consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform) checks with Google whether consent is required where you are. The Mobile Ads SDK starts only when that tool says ads may be requested (based on this check or on the choice you made in an earlier session). Once started, the SDK may collect your IP address, device and app identifiers (including the Android advertising ID and App Set ID), app interaction data, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . How Google uses information in ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

  • Ad partner: Google AdMob.
  • Ad types: rewarded ads only, shown only when you choose to watch one (for example for an in-game boost).
  • Test builds: test versions of Knoll (such as closed tests on Google Play) show Google's test ads. The data handling described here is the same.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: where consent is required, Knoll shows Google's consent message. Only players who consent get personalized ads. If you do not consent, you get non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Knoll under Settings > About > Privacy options (open Settings from the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen, or from the pause menu). The Privacy options button appears only where Google's consent tool says privacy options are required.
  • Your consent choice is stored on your device, not on Canoe Development servers. If Android backup is on, it is included in your device backup.
  • Everyone else, including the US: Knoll requests non-personalized ads only. Non-personalized ads are not based on your past behavior, but Google still uses the data above for frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Privacy Sandbox: Knoll removes the Android ad-services Topics and Attribution permissions, so the ads SDK cannot use those APIs in Knoll.

6. Purchases

  • Knoll sells nothing and processes no payments. It includes no billing library. The in-game Store uses tokens you earn in the game; token packs and "Remove ads" are shown as not for sale and cannot be bought.
  • If purchases are added later, Google Play Billing will process payments. Canoe Development will update this policy first.

7. How data is used

Data processed through Knoll is used to:

  • Run the game and keep your progress on your device.
  • Save and restore your game progress through your Play Games Saved Games, if you are signed in.
  • Show rewarded ads through Google AdMob (Section 5).
  • Reply to you if you email us.

Canoe Development does not sell personal data. Google receives data through the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in Section 5. Outside the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, including the US, Knoll requests only non-personalized ads.

8. Sharing

  • Canoe Development does not share personal data it holds with third parties.
  • Google processes data through Play Games Services as described in Section 4.
  • Google receives data through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described in Section 5.
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

9. Storage and retention

  • Game data on your device (progress, settings such as sound and vibration, and your consent choice) stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall Knoll. If Android backup is on, Android may back up this app data to your own Google account. Google handles that backup under its own terms. You control backup in your device settings.
  • Cloud saves are stored by Google in your Google account. Retention follows Google's policies and your Play Games settings.
  • Emails you send us are kept for up to 12 months after your question is resolved, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

10. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Play without Play Games: If you already use Play Games, it may sign you in automatically. You can manage this in your Play Games settings. Without Play Games sign-in, nothing is saved to the cloud and your progress stays on your device.
  • Ads choices: Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, change your consent in Knoll under Settings > About > Privacy options.
  • Device backup: Turn Android backup on or off in your device settings.
  • Reset progress in the app: Settings > Reset progress > Erase progress erases your Knoll progress on the device and in your Play Games save (completed levels, stars, scores, talent points and ranks, and any level in progress). It keeps your tokens and Store items and your sound and vibration settings.
  • Delete all local data: Clear Knoll's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall Knoll.
  • Delete your cloud save: Delete Knoll's Play Games data from your Google account: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646 . You can also manage your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com .
  • Ask us: Email canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com to ask about your data or to request deletion of anything Canoe Development holds (in practice, only emails you have sent us).

We will reply to deletion requests within 30 days. Canoe Development holds no game data on its own servers, so game data is deleted with the controls above.

11. Children

Knoll is a puzzle game for players aged 13 and older. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13. Knoll does not ask for your age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, email canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs in Knoll is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

13. International users

Knoll is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data on servers in other countries under its own policies. Players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are asked for ad consent as described in Section 5.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to how it's used. Email canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com to make a request. For data Google holds, use the Google controls in Section 10.
