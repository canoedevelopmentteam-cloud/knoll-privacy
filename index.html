1. Who we are

Knoll is a game made by Canoe Development ("Canoe", "we", "us"). [LEGAL ENTITY NAME, once formed] [POSTAL ADDRESS]. Contact: canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com. This policy explains what data the Knoll Android app handles. It covers the Knoll app for Android distributed on Google Play.

Questions? Email canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • Knoll has no account system of its own. We do not ask you to create a Canoe account.
  • Knoll uses Google Play Games Services for cloud save only. Cloud save works only if you are signed in to Play Games. You can play without it, and your progress then stays on your device.
  • Google processes data when you use Play Games Services. Google's own privacy policy covers that.
  • Gameplay data like Lives is stored on your device, and in your cloud save if you are signed in to Play Games.
  • Knoll offers no in-app purchases and does not charge you money.
  • Knoll shows ads through Google AdMob. The app includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect device identifiers (including your advertising ID), your IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data. The ads SDK starts only after Knoll's consent check, which runs each time the app opens. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, you are asked for consent, and only players who consent get personalized ads. Everyone else, including players in the US, gets non-personalized ads. See Section 5.

4. What Google processes (Play Games Services)

Knoll uses Google Play Games Services to save your game progress to the cloud ("Saved Games").

When you are signed in to Play Games (Knoll may sign you in automatically if you already use a Play Games profile), Google may process data such as:

  • Your Play Games player ID and gamer identity (gamer name and avatar), plus analytics and diagnostics data that Google's Play Games SDK collects automatically.
  • Your saved game data.
  • Device and service information Google needs to run the service.

Your saved-game data is stored in your Google account. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Play Games profile settings you choose.

Canoe does not control how Google processes this data.

Knoll does not use Play Games achievements, leaderboards, or friends features.

5. Ads

Knoll shows ads through Google AdMob, Google's ad network. Knoll includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Each time Knoll opens, it first checks whether ad consent is needed (see below). Only after that check does this SDK start, and it may then collect your IP address, device and app identifiers (including the Android advertising ID and App Set ID), app interaction data, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . How Google uses information in ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

  • Ad partner: Google AdMob.
  • Ad types: rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: Knoll shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform). Only players who consent get personalized ads. If you do not consent, you get non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in [IN-APP PRIVACY SETTINGS LOCATION].
  • Consent check and your choice: Each time Knoll opens, Google's consent tool contacts Google to work out whether consent is required where you are. Your consent choice is stored on your device, not on Canoe servers. If Android backup is on, it is included in your device backup.
  • Everyone else, including the US: Knoll shows non-personalized ads only. Non-personalized ads are not based on your past behavior, but Google still uses the data above for frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention.

6. Purchases

  • Knoll offers no in-app purchases and processes no payments.
  • If purchases are added later, Google Play Billing will process payments. Canoe will update this policy first.

7. How data is used

Data processed through Knoll is used to:

  • Save your game progress to your Play Games Saved Games (via Google Play Games).
  • Run the game on your device (for example, Lives timers).
  • Show ads through Google AdMob (Section 5).
  • Reply to you if you email us.

Canoe does not sell personal data for money. Google receives data through the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in Section 5. In the US, Knoll requests only non-personalized ads.

8. Sharing

  • Canoe does not share personal data it holds with third parties.
  • Google processes data through Play Games Services as described in Section 4.
  • Google receives data through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described in Section 5.

9. Storage and retention

  • Game data on your device stays there until you clear app data or uninstall. If Android backup is on, Android may back up your game progress to your own Google account. Google handles that backup under its own terms. You control backup in your device settings.
  • Cloud saves are stored by Google. Retention follows Google's policies and your Play Games settings.
  • Emails you send us are kept for up to 12 months after your question is resolved, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

10. Your choices

  • Play without Play Games: If you already use Play Games, Knoll may sign you in automatically. You can manage this in your Play Games settings. Without Play Games sign-in, nothing is saved to the cloud and your progress stays on your device.
  • Ads choices: Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, change your consent in [IN-APP PRIVACY SETTINGS LOCATION].
  • Device backup: Turn Android backup on or off in your device settings.
  • Delete local data: Clear app storage or uninstall Knoll.
  • Delete cloud save / Play Games data: Follow Google's instructions to delete your Knoll game data: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646 . You can also manage your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com .
  • Ask us: Email canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com to ask about your data or request deletion of anything Canoe holds.

We will reply to deletion requests within 30 days. Canoe holds no game data on its own servers, so most requests are handled through the Google controls above.

11. Children

Knoll is a puzzle game for players aged 13 and older. It is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, email canoedevelopmentteam@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs in Knoll is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
